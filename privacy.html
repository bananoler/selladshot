4. Server log files

The provider of the pages automatically collects and stores information in server log files, which your browser transmits automatically. These are:

  • browser type and version
  • operating system used
  • referrer URL
  • host name of the accessing computer
  • time of the server request
  • IP address

This data is not merged with other data sources. The collection is based on Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR. The website operator has a legitimate interest in the technically error-free presentation and optimisation of its website.

5. Cookies, fonts and videos

This website does not use tracking or marketing cookies and does not use analytics services. Fonts and videos are delivered from our own server; no data is transmitted to Google, YouTube or other third parties for this purpose.

6. Contacting us

If you send us your project details by e-mail, phone, WhatsApp or Instagram, this data is stored by us to process your enquiry and in case of follow-up questions. We do not pass this data on without your consent. Processing is based on Art. 6 (1)(b) GDPR where your enquiry relates to the performance of a contract or is necessary to take pre-contractual steps, and otherwise on Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR.

You can ask me a question through the chat assistant on this website. The assistant itself only answers predefined questions and does not store any input. If you submit the contact form in the chat, your question and your e-mail address are forwarded to me by e-mail via the form service FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) and used solely to answer your enquiry. Processing is based on your consent (Art. 6 (1)(a) GDPR), which you give before submitting and can withdraw at any time.

The links to WhatsApp (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.) and Instagram (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.) are simple links. Data is only transmitted to the respective provider once you click them; their processing is governed by the provider’s privacy policy.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority, e.g. the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

8. Storage period

Unless a more specific storage period is stated in this privacy policy, your personal data remains with us until the purpose of the processing ceases to apply. If you make a legitimate request for deletion or withdraw consent you have given, your data will be deleted, unless there are other legally permissible grounds for storing it (e.g. tax or commercial-law retention periods for invoice data).
