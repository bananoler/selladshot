About me

I’m Denys, a video editor and content producer who turns complex products into clear, simple videos that people actually want to watch.

I combine real filming and editing with AI, so I can create detailed animations that show exactly how a product works, much faster and for far less money than 3D modelling, and mix them with real footage so the video still feels like your business.

I work mostly with product brands, manufacturers and trades businesses, making explainers, ads, short-form Reels and TikToks, and AI video and photography. Every project starts with a clear price and a clear process.

AI avatar

concept, filming, AI generation, editing

Challenge

Show what an AI avatar can do: turn a real person on camera into any character, in the same shot, with no costume and no studio.

Idea

I filmed myself talking to the camera at home and replaced my head with an AI-generated horse. The body, the light and the room stay real, so the character feels like it’s actually in the shot. The same method works for a brand mascot or a product character that talks to your customers.

Result

Real shot, AI character, zero studio, in various locations, languages possible.
